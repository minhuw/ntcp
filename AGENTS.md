# Agent instructions

1. You may generate documents for your own or other agents' use, but must never author documents intended for human readers.
2. Store agent-use documents, plans, drafts, and notes in `workbench/`. This directory is ignored by Git; do not force-add its contents or link to them from tracked human-facing documents.
3. Reserve `docs/` for human-facing documents. Do not place agent-generated documents there.
